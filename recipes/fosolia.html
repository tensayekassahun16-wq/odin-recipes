Fosolia

Description

Fosolia (or fasolia) is a mild, flavorful Ethiopian stir-fry made primarily from green beans, carrots, and onions

Ingredients

  • Green beans: 1 pound (450g), trimmed and halved.
  • Carrots: 3 medium, cut into thin matchsticks.
  • Onion: 1 large, thinly sliced.
  • Garlic: 3 cloves, minced.
  • Ginger: 1 tablespoon, minced or grated.
  • Turmeric: ½ teaspoon.
  • Cooking oil: 2 tablespoons (or niter kibbeh for authentic flavor).
  • Salt and pepper: To taste.
  • Jalapeño: 1, sliced (optional, for mild heat).

Stpes

  1. Parboil the vegetables: Boil the green beans and carrots in salted water for 5 minutes until slightly tender. Drain and set aside.
  2. Sauté the aromatics: Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook the onions for 5 minutes until soft.
  3. Add flavor: Stir in the garlic, ginger, and turmeric. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes until fragrant.
  4. Combine everything: Add the drained green beans, carrots, and jalapeño (if using) to the skillet.
  5. Simmer and soften: Cover the pan and turn the heat to low. Let it cook for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables are very tender.
  6. Season: Add salt and black pepper to taste. Serve warm.
